# About MiMinions

We're building the future of autonomous AI agents. Our mission is to make AI
agents accessible, safe, and powerful for developers worldwide.

!!! quote "Our Vision"

    To democratise AI agent development and create a world where autonomous AI
    systems can safely and effectively assist in a wide range of tasks. We
    believe agentic AI will transform how we work, create, and solve problems.

!!! note "Our Mission"

    Providing developers with the tools and framework they need to build
    reliable, safe, and powerful AI agents. We're committed to open-source
    development, community collaboration, and pushing the boundaries of what's
    possible with autonomous AI.

## What we do

- :material-check-circle: Build open-source AI agent frameworks
- :material-check-circle: Develop multi-agent orchestration systems
- :material-check-circle: Create safety mechanisms for autonomous AI
- :material-check-circle: Provide tools for production AI deployment
